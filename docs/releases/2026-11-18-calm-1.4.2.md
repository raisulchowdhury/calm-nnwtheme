# Calm 1.4.2

Calm 1.4.2 fixes footnotes and gets ready for NetNewsWire 7.3.

## Fixes

- Footnote popovers are readable again. NetNewsWire places the popover inside
  the footnote's superscript, and Calm's superscript styles collapsed every
  line of the popover onto one. The popover now keeps body-sized text and line
  height, and uses Calm's paper, rule, and shadow in light and dark
  appearances. Thanks to @danieldickison for the report (#13).
- Clicking a section on the reading map now marks that section as current.
  Before, the map often highlighted and labeled the section above it.
- The compact paragraph wrapping fix from August now ships under its own
  version number. Reinstalling picks it up for anyone who installed before
  August 30.

## NetNewsWire 7.3

- Adds `template7.3.html`. NetNewsWire 7.3 and later show an article's primary
  image, audio, or video above the body and its other enclosures below it,
  inside Calm's reading measure. Older versions keep using `template.html`.
- The reading map watches the whole article, so late-loading media refreshes
  its markers.
