# Calm 1.4.0

Calm 1.4 sharpens the reading hierarchy and makes the wider-screen reading map
more useful without adding persistent chrome.

## Highlights

- A larger, more clearly separated article title on rail-capable layouts.
- The reading map now sits just outside the centered article column.
- Feed identity remains visible in the opening metadata, with estimated reading
  time shown only for articles of four minutes or longer.
- Missing metadata fields collapse cleanly without leaving stray separators.
- The persistent statistics footer and visible word count are gone.
- Structured articles can reveal the active section name on wide Mac layouts
  during hover, keyboard focus, or scrubbing.
- iPad keeps marker-only feedback with 44-point targets.
- Phone, Split View, and short coarse-pointer landscape layouts remain
  completely rail-free.
- Entering or leaving iPad Split View now tears down or restores the reading map
  and its interaction listeners without requiring an article reload.

Calm’s article measure, serif body rhythm, light and dark palettes, media
treatment, body-heading scale, keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, and
Tap + Scrub interaction are unchanged.
