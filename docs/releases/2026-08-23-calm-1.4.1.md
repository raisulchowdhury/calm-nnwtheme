# Calm 1.4.1

Calm 1.4.1 is a fundamentals release. It fixes compact iPhone typography,
preserves authored image colors, and makes the reading map more selective,
efficient, and accessible without adding reader chrome.

## Reading fixes

- Corrected the compact cascade so the one-point type bump and `1.48` line
  height take effect without flattening NetNewsWire text-size preferences.
- Removed light-mode `mix-blend-mode: multiply` from article images and SVGs.

## Reading map

- Hides the map for articles shorter than two viewport heights while continuing
  to watch for images or other late content that makes the article long enough.
- Hardens the existing structured-map selection around a coherent heading
  hierarchy, filters duplicate and common utility headings, and caps the result
  at eight body destinations.
- Throttles scroll work to animation frames and caches heading offsets until a
  real layout change occurs.
- Refreshes geometry after resize, embedded-media load, or observed content
  growth.
- Applies `aria-current="location"` to the active marker.
- Cancels queued work and removes observers and listeners during compact Split
  View teardown, then reconstructs cleanly when space returns.

Markers remain evenly spaced in 1.4.1. Proportional placement should only ship
after observation with real readers shows that it improves orientation.

## Validation

The release adds WebKit coverage for compact typography, light-mode images,
short and long articles, mixed heading hierarchies, image/video/audio fixtures,
media-driven reflow, Split View reconstruction, and active-marker
accessibility. A canonical Chromium screenshot covers visual comparisons.
