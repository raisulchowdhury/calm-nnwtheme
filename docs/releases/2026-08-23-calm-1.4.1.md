# Calm 1.4.1

Calm 1.4.1 is a fundamentals release. It fixes compact iPhone typography,
preserves authored image colors, and makes the reading map more selective,
efficient, and accessible without adding reader chrome.

## Reading fixes

- Corrected a WebKit cascade that allowed the desktop `1.6` line height to
  override the intended compact `1.48` rhythm.
- Removed light-mode `mix-blend-mode: multiply` from article images and SVGs.

## Reading map

- Hides the map for articles shorter than two viewport heights.
- Builds structured maps from a coherent heading hierarchy, filters duplicate
  and common utility headings, and caps the result at eight body destinations.
- Throttles scroll work to animation frames and caches heading offsets until a
  real layout change occurs.
- Refreshes geometry after resize, embedded-media load, or observed content
  growth.
- Applies `aria-current="location"` to the active marker.
- Cancels queued work and removes observers and listeners during compact Split
  View teardown, then reconstructs cleanly when space returns.

Markers remain evenly spaced in 1.4.1. Proportional placement should only ship
after observation with real readers shows that it improves orientation.

## Validation

The release adds browser coverage in WebKit for compact typography, light-mode
images, short and long articles, mixed heading hierarchies, media-driven reflow,
Split View reconstruction, active-marker accessibility, and canonical visual
comparisons.
