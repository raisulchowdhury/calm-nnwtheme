# Calm 1.4.3

Calm 1.4.3 makes the reading map reach the end of every article and lets
iPhone and iPad text follow your system text size.

## Fixes

- When an article's last few sections all fit on its final screen, each one
  now gets its own place on the reading map, so clicking or scrolling to it
  marks it as current. Before, only the last could ever be current.
- The "Bottom" marker on articles without headings now scrolls to the true
  end of the article instead of stopping about 96px short.

## iPhone and iPad

- Article text now follows the system text size (Dynamic Type) on iPhone and
  iPad, like NetNewsWire's own theme. At the default size Calm looks the same
  as before. On the Mac, Calm keeps following NetNewsWire's text size setting.
