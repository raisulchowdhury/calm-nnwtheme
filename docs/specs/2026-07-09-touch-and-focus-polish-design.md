# Touch and Focus Polish

## Objective

Improve Calm's interaction quality without changing its editorial character or responsive layout.

## Design

- Preserve the current phone, iPad portrait, Split View, and full-width iPad landscape behavior.
- Give the section rail a visible keyboard focus ring that follows the system accent color and appears only for keyboard focus.
- On compact layouts, make the article title, metadata links, and disclosure summary at least 44 points tall while preserving the existing visual rhythm.
- On touch-capable wide layouts, enlarge rail controls to 44-point targets without changing the position or appearance of their visual markers.
- Use the established muted color for reading statistics so the small footer text remains legible in light and dark appearances.

## Validation

- Automated checks protect the focus treatment, touch-target rules, responsive rail behavior, footer color, synchronized scripts, and installable archive.
- Verify the preview at phone, iPad portrait, iPad Split View, and iPad landscape dimensions in light and dark appearances.
