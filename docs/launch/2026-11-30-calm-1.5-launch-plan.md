# Calm 1.5 launch plan

Status: draft for Raisul. Nothing here is posted until he says so.

## Goal

Get people who already use Calm onto 1.5, and get it in front of
NetNewsWire users who haven't tried it. A good week is 1,500 downloads of
the 1.5.0 release, which would match 1.4.1 and 1.4.3.

## What we know

Checked on 2026-11-27.

- **Where downloads came from.** 1.4.1 had 1,618 downloads and 1.4.3 had
  1,416. 1.4.4 has 222 so far. The two sources were the
  [theme marketplace](https://dave-atx.github.io/nnw-theme-marketplace/) and
  Brent Simmons's post
  ["New Theme: Calm"](https://netnewswire.blog/2026/11/20/new-theme-calm.html)
  on the NetNewsWire blog on Nov 20.
- **The marketplace ranks by downloads in the last 7 days.** A release that
  gets its downloads in one week climbs; the same downloads over a month
  don't.
- **Only 1.4.4 shows the update line.** About 222 installs will be told
  about 1.5 inside NetNewsWire. Everyone on 1.4.3 or older has to hear some
  other way.
- **Where NetNewsWire people are.** The forum's Themes category, where the
  [Calm thread](https://discourse.netnewswire.com/t/new-theme-calm/509) has
  553 views and a reply from Brent, and Mastodon, where @NetNewsWire has
  3,427 followers. Slack, GitHub Discussions and Bluesky are quiet. Reddit
  and X weren't checked.
- **What worked for another theme.** Ringo posted "what's new since 1.8" as
  a reply in its own thread and that thread has 4,569 views. Daisies opened
  a new topic for its update and got 93.
- **No press covers NetNewsWire themes.** Pitching writers isn't worth the
  time.
- **Timing.** US Thanksgiving weekend ends Sunday Nov 29.

## The plan

Raisul chose on 2026-11-27 to release Saturday Nov 28 and post Monday
Nov 30. The quiet weekend works as a soft launch: 1.4.4 users see the update
line, and a broken feed can get a 1.5.1 before the posts go up.

### Forum reply, in the Calm thread

Same order as the gallery and the release notes: the five highlights, then
one line for the rest. It follows the shape of Ringo's "what's new since
1.8" reply in its own thread (seven bold-led lines, one picture, one link).
Raisul's forum account is at trust level 0, where Discourse by default
allows one picture and two links in a post, so the draft uses exactly that.
This forum's own limit wasn't readable; if the composer accepts more, add
the single before and after pictures under their lines.

> Calm 1.5 is out. It's the biggest update since I posted this thread.
> What's new, the things you'll notice most first:
>
> - **One quiet button for sign-ups.** An email form can't send from a
>   reader, so it becomes one button to the publisher's sign-up page.
> - **No file names under podcasts.** The caption shows the length when the
>   feed gives it.
> - **Substack's buttons under pictures are gone** (since 1.4.4).
> - **Photo galleries stay in a row** instead of stacking full width.
> - **Quiet link cards.** A linked post from Substack, Ghost or WordPress is
>   one card instead of loose text and buttons.
> - **X posts follow dark mode.**
> - **Footnotes open in the text.** Tap the number and the note opens under
>   that line.
>
> [1 picture: `assets/releases/1.5.0-highlights.gif`, a loop of five of these, each before and after]
>
> Also: a repeated cover picture shows once, plain captions look like
> captions, the "appeared first on" line is gone, untitled Mastodon posts
> start at the date, and code gets a Copy button on the Mac. Headings and
> quotes read a little more clearly too.
>
> The full list, with pictures:
> https://github.com/raisulchowdhury/calm-nnwtheme/releases/tag/v1.5.0
>
> To update, open https://raisul.xyz/calm on the device, tap Install in
> NetNewsWire and choose Overwrite.
>
> If an article looks wrong in Calm, send me the feed and I'll fix it.
