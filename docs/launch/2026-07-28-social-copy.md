## LinkedIn

Not everything is meant to be scrolled.

I made Calm, a NetNewsWire theme that helps you navigate long articles without
distraction.

On Mac and iPad, Calm adds a reading map outside the article column. Structured
pieces become destinations; simpler essays get five quiet markers. You can tap,
click, or scrub to move through the article.

On iPhone and compact layouts, the map disappears entirely.

https://raisul.xyz/projects/calm

Calm is open source and available now:
https://github.com/raisulchowdhury/calm-nnwtheme
